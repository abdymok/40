# Kramatorsk Oct. 9 fatality — unresolved OHCHR lead

**Status:** Not entered as an incident record; local source not yet identified.

The UN Human Rights Monitoring Mission in Ukraine's October 2026 report states that on Oct. 9 Russian FPV drone strikes caused fires in two nine-storey residential buildings in Kramatorsk and killed one man.

Source:
- [OHCHR October 2026 civilian casualty report (PDF)](https://ukraine.un.org/sites/default/files/2026-11/Ukraine%20-%20protection%20of%20civilians%20in%20armed%20conflict%20%28October%29_ENG_0.pdf)

Searches of Ukrainian official and independent reporting located:
- Oct. 5: a separate FPV drone attack on ambulances and a residential building in Kramatorsk, killing a 49-year-old woman.
- Oct. 8: the separate strike on two passenger buses in Kramatorsk, with a locally reported final toll of 33.
- Oct. 20: the separate attack that killed three municipal heating-network workers and one additional person.

No Ukrainian regional-administration, prosecutor, police, DSNS or major national-media report has yet been identified for a distinct Oct. 9 Kramatorsk fatality.

**Current treatment:** Do not add a death until the Oct. 9 event is corroborated and distinguished from the Oct. 5, Oct. 8 and Oct. 20 incidents. Keep it as an explicit unresolved lead.
