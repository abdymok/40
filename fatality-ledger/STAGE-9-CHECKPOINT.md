## Important discrepancy: Dnipro, Nov. 3

The current methodology paragraph records one death in the Nov. 3 morning attack on Dnipro and adds only one to the running minimum.

The source sequence is:
- At 09:28, Dnipropetrovsk Regional Military Administration head Oleksandr Hanzha reported one person killed.
- At 13:38, after the search-and-rescue operation, Hanzha reported **two dead and 20 injured**. Ukrainska Pravda published the final update.

The incident record therefore has two documented deaths, with a preserved revision from 1 to 2. The current method's Nov. 3 arithmetic appears to add only one. Unless another project entry already included the second Dnipro death, this is a one-death omission in the running subtotal. It must be checked against the Nov. 3 chronology and the subsequent cumulative arithmetic before any public total is changed.

Sources:
- [Interfax-Ukraine, Nov. 3, 09:57](https://en.interfax.com.ua/news/general/1230254.html) — initial one-death report.
- [Ukrainska Pravda, Nov. 3, 13:38](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2026/11/03/8061027/) — completed rescue operation, two deaths.
