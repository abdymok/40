# Stage 118 checkpoint — Kramatorsk Oct. 8 casualty-source reconciliation

Date: Nov. 27, 2026

## Question

The Oct. 11 ≈80 selected-incident snapshot labels 33 deaths as the Kramatorsk bus strike. Later records separate the Oct. 8 casualties into the bus strike, a Bilenke attack and an evening FPV strike on a civilian car. The issue is whether the snapshot's 33-death bus row can be used to establish inclusion of the separate Bilenke or car deaths.

## Source review

- [Donetsk Regional Prosecutor's Office update, via Ukrinform, Oct. 8, 12:36](https://www.ukrinform.net/rubric-ato/4172298-death-toll-from-russian-strike-on-two-buses-in-kramatorsk-rises-to-33-18-injured.html): 30 killed and 18 injured in the bus strike.
- [State Emergency Service figure, via Interfax-Ukraine, Oct. 8, 13:21](https://en.interfax.com.ua/news/general/1216402.html): 33 killed and 18 injured in the bus strike. The same report separately notes three people killed in Bilenke.
- [Kramatorsk City Council, Oct. 9, 18:53](https://krm.gov.ua/mynuloyi-doby-8-zhovtnya-kramatorsk-zaznav-18-obstriliv-35-zagyblyh-ta-22-poranenyh/): incident-by-incident itemization gives 30 killed in the 10:00 bus strike, three killed in the separate 10:10 Bilenke strike and two killed in the separate 18:40 FPV strike on a civilian car. The citywide Kramatorsk total is 32, with three additional deaths in Bilenke.
- [Donetsk Regional State Administration, Oct. 9, 10:00](https://dn.gov.ua/en/news/operatyvna-sytuatsiia-po-oblasti-stanom-na-ranok-9-zhovtnia-09-10-26): regional summary reports 32 killed in Kramatorsk and three in Bilenke. This is consistent with the City Council's 30 bus plus two car deaths in Kramatorsk, and three separate Bilenke deaths.
- [Suspilne Donbas, Oct. 26, citing the Kramatorsk District Police response](https://suspilne.media/donbas/1438409-sered-zagiblih-e-ne-vstanovleni-v-policii-doneccini-povidomili-dani-sodo-zertv-udaru-po-avtobusah-v-kramatorsku/): the police investigation established 30 killed and 20 injured in the bus strike. Some victims remained unidentified.

## Finding

The contemporaneous official sources conflict on the bus-strike toll: the Prosecutor's Office reported 30, while the SES later reported 33. The SES report explicitly described 33 as bus-strike deaths and separately noted three deaths in Bilenke. The later City Council itemization and police response establish 30 bus deaths, three in Bilenke and two in the evening car strike. The Oct. 9 regional aggregate independently reports 32 in Kramatorsk and three in Bilenke.

The Oct. 11 snapshot's 33-death bus row may reflect the SES figure. It does not establish whether the three Bilenke deaths or two evening-car deaths were separately included in the ≈80 subtotal. The three-person discrepancy between the official bus figures remains unexplained. It must not be reassigned to Bilenke without evidence.

## Adjudication

- Bus-strike final toll remains 30. The 30 deaths remain dated Oct. 11 as part of the reported 33 in the selected subtotal.
- The SES 33 is retained as a superseded conflicting official report, not a separate casualty addition.
- Bilenke remains three deaths with `ledger_effective_at: null`. Historical inclusion in the Oct. 11 or later subtotals is unresolved.
- The evening car strike remains two deaths with `ledger_effective_at: null`. Historical inclusion in the later 148/421 carry-forward is unresolved.
- No deaths are added to or subtracted from the public subtotal.
