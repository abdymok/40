This is provisional because the Uman death may or may not already be in 148. These comparison adjustments are not proposed ledger additions. The ledger remains at 446 overall and the Chabany exclusion remains in force unless the user changes that instruction.

The same scope adjustments carry forward into the published 418 total. The raw current-ledger difference is +28 (446 versus 418). A provisional like-for-like comparison is 450 versus 418, or +32. Do not interpret 450 as an approved count. The current ledger remains 446.
