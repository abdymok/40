## Evidence interpretation

- The count of 11 is the final reported toll when the rescue operation concluded on Nov. 23.
- The 79-year-old woman was alive when rescued at 03:28 on Nov. 23. She must not be added to the toll until the Nov. 24 report of her death in hospital.
- The 12th death is therefore a new fatality confirmation on Nov. 24, not a correction to the 11 bodies recovered by the rescue operation.
- People listed as missing, people injured and survivors rescued alive are not deaths. Do not infer deaths from missing-person counts.
- The current source set supports 12 documented deaths. No source reviewed for this matrix establishes a 13th death.
