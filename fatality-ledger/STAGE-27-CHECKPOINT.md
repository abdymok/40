## Key implication

At the Oct. 26 cutoff, the current incident file already sums to 157, while the public running subtotal immediately before the Oct. 27 increments is described as approximately 141. That is a 16-death difference at this earlier point. By Oct. 27, the timestamped ledger sum is 160 and the historical subtotal is approximately 146, a 14-death difference. The difference remains 14 on Oct. 28 (162 versus 148).

This demonstrates that the 14-death gap was not created solely by the Oct. 29 transition. It was already present in the pre-Oct. 29 inventory and was narrowed by two deaths that were present in the current file's timestamped values but were apparently not yet reflected in the historical running subtotal at the Oct. 26 cutoff.

## Important caution

The current recorded_at values are not ledger_effective_at values. A revision entered into the structured ledger on Oct. 26 may have been incorporated into the public running subtotal later, or not at all. The Oct. 27 narrative is also an approximate running figure, not a formal list of every incident included. Therefore, the 16- and 14-death differences are diagnostic, not a confirmed list of omitted incidents.
