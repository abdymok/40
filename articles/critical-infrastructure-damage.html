Oct. 2South Bridge / Kyiv bridge networkAt about 5 a.m., a Russian UAV struck the South (Pivdennyi) Bridge again. Kyiv authorities subsequently reported that the bridge was fully closed to traffic in both directions. Police also reported partial restriction to one lane on Metro Bridge from the left bank toward the right bank, and a complete restriction on Paton Bridge for traffic from the right bank toward the left bank. These are the current bridge-traffic restrictions reported by the Kyiv City Military Administration. The extent of new physical damage to the South Bridge remains under assessment. Separately, debris from the overnight attack damaged windows in a non-residential building and several cars in Holosiivskyi district; the initial report cited no fire or casualties at that location.
Oct. 1South Bridge / Green Line metroRussian drones struck the South (Pivdennyi) Bridge area repeatedly. Two impacts were reported near the support structure, followed later by a strike on the roadway. Reported damage included a bridge support, crash barrier, roadway and metro fence. Metro and surface transport across the bridge were halted during inspections. Surface public transport later resumed, while metro-related inspections continued. Initial reporting said specialists found no critical structural damage in the preliminary inspection; this did not establish the status after the further strike or the renewed attack on Oct. 2. Reports identifying the drones as Geran-5 were not officially confirmed.
Sept. 30Kyiv energy infrastructureRussia launched a large-scale missile and drone attack on Ukraine's energy infrastructure, with power facilities around Kyiv among the reported targets. The attack caused power outages and disrupted other critical services in the capital. Ukrainian officials said critical infrastructure was hit in several regions. The full extent of damage to individual Kyiv generation facilities was not publicly identified.
Sept. 30Darnytska CHP / energy generationDarnytska combined heat and power plant has sustained significant damage from Russian shelling during the war. The plant supplies heat to more than 1,100 residential buildings in Kyiv's Darnytsky and Dniprovsky districts. This entry is included as a major Kyiv generation facility affected by Russian attacks, with the damage and restoration status requiring continuing updates.
