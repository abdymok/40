# Stage 116 checkpoint — Kramatorsk, Oct. 8 incident separation

Date: Nov. 27, 2026

## Three distinct incidents

Kramatorsk City Council's Oct. 8 memorial statement and Oct. 9 incident-by-incident recap distinguish three attacks on Oct. 8:

1. **10:00 — central Kramatorsk bus strike:** 30 people killed and 20 injured. The City Council's Oct. 9 recap gives 30. A later response from Kramatorsk District Police, obtained by Suspilne Donbas and dated Oct. 26, states that the investigation established 30 killed and 20 injured. OHCHR records 11 verified deaths plus 19 additional reported deaths pending verification, also totalling 30.
2. **10:10 — Bilenke:** three people killed in a separate FAB strike. The City Council's memorial statement says three were killed in Bilenke; its Oct. 9 recap lists a 10:10 strike by two FAB-250 bombs and one FAB-500 that killed two men born in 1975 and 1976 and a woman born in 1955. Suspilne Donbas also reports the three Bilenke deaths, citing the city military administration head.
3. **18:40 — central Kramatorsk:** an FPV drone struck a civilian car, killing two men born in 1979 and 1987. The City Council's two updates and Suspilne Donbas distinguish this from the bus strike.

The community-wide toll reported by the City Council is 35: 30 bus victims + 3 Bilenke victims + 2 car victims.

The bus incident record is corrected to 30, and Bilenke is recorded as its own three-death incident. The earlier 30→33 bus revision is retained as a superseded, overinclusive report with null effective date; the correction 33→30 is also null and does not reduce the public subtotal.

Both records are assigned the first documented inclusion date, Oct. 11. This is a decomposition of the existing 33, not an additional +3.
