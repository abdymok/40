# Stage 66 checkpoint — Lozova market revision and separate warehouse death

Date: 26 November 2026

## Scope

This stage follows up two Lozova-region incident records that Stage 54 left unresolved in the historical dashboard sequence:

- The Oct. 22 strike on Lozova central market: two women killed initially; a third woman died in hospital on Oct. 23.
- A separate Oct. 24 missile strike on a Lozova warehouse: one man killed.

The current ledger has separate records for both incidents:
- `UA-KHA-LOZOVA-2026-10-22-CENTRAL-MARKET`: final local toll 3.
- `UA-KHA-LOZOVA-2026-10-24-WAREHOUSE-MISSILE`: 1 death.

## Historical inclusion check

The Oct. 26 dashboard transition from approximately 132 to 137 explicitly itemizes five additions: one in Marhanets/Nikopol district, two initial deaths at Lozova central market, one in Konotop district and one in Yarova. The update text therefore establishes inclusion of the market's initial two deaths, not the later third death.

The market's third death was reported on Oct. 23. The current ledger cites Interfax-Ukraine's Oct. 23, 09:58 report for the revised toll. The historical Oct. 26 +5 update does not mention that third death, and no later mapped transition in Stage 54 identifies it as an addition. Its inclusion in any other subtotal cannot be established from the reviewed dashboard diffs.

The separate warehouse strike occurred on Oct. 24. Interfax-Ukraine and Ukrinform reports cited in the current ledger were published on Oct. 24 at approximately 14:47 and 14:35, respectively. The Oct. 25 transition from approximately 123 to 132 explicitly itemizes nine deaths from Synelnykove (4), Kharkiv (1) and four other regional deaths on Oct. 23. It does not itemize the Oct. 24 Lozova warehouse death. The Oct. 26 +5 and subsequent Oct. 26–27 transitions mapped in Stage 54 do not identify it either.

## Finding and uncertainty

The reviewed historical transitions account explicitly for the first two Lozova market deaths but do not account explicitly for:
1. The third market death, reported on Oct. 23.
2. The separate warehouse death, reported on Oct. 24.

These are two candidate omissions from the historical nationwide documented minimum sequence, not proof that the deaths were excluded from every subtotal. The Oct. 25/26 dashboards used approximate nationwide minimums, and their update histories are not a full incident-level ledger. It remains possible that one or both deaths were already absorbed into an earlier or broader subtotal without an explicit itemization. No source reviewed here establishes that.

Do not simply add +2 to any historical subtotal. Do not assign either death an effective date from the current ledger's incident date or from a source publication date. Keep the two incidents separate.

## Integrity

- The Oct. 26 132→137 update explicitly includes two Lozova market deaths, not three.
- The Oct. 23 report of the third market death predates the Oct. 25/26 updates but is not explicitly mapped in their itemized additions.
- The Oct. 24 warehouse death was reported before the Oct. 25/26 updates but is not explicitly mapped in their itemized additions.
- Current ledger records and final documented tolls remain unchanged.
- Public subtotal 421 remains unchanged.
- No public chronology entry or effective date changed.
