## Structural finding

The current site does not yet have a machine-readable incident ledger. Fatality information is embedded in long prose paragraphs in `method/index.html`, repeated in chronology entries, and summarized again in `articles/changes.html`. The dashboard number is hard-coded in the chronology HTML. The same event can therefore have several separately maintained descriptions and cumulative figures.

The existing SOP already states that dashboard counts should be the single source of truth and that methodology notes should reference dashboard values. The implementation does not currently meet that standard.

## Dymer: established record in the current files

The Nov. 22 Russian strike on a five-storey residential building in Dymer is represented by one chronology incident and a Nov. 24 follow-up:

- The chronology describes the toll rising to six on Nov. 22, then seven at 22:52.
- On Nov. 23, the regional administration updates report the eighth and ninth bodies at 02:15 and 02:38; a 79-year-old woman is rescued alive at 03:28; the tenth and eleventh bodies are reported at 05:51 and 07:23.
- The Nov. 24 follow-up says the rescued 79-year-old woman died in hospital, taking the incident toll from 11 to 12.
- Current chronology sources include Tkachenko Telegram posts 3138–3143 and 3145, plus Censor.NET. The main incident entry links posts 3139–3143; the follow-up links 3145.

**Working incident-level conclusion for reconstruction:** 12 documented deaths are recorded by the current chronology, subject to source verification. The woman rescued alive is not counted on Nov. 23; her death is added only on Nov. 24. The available page text does not establish a thirteenth death.

## Material inconsistencies found

1. The Nov. 23 change-log entry says Dymer rose from nine to 11 and raises the cumulative minimum from 392 to 394.
2. A separate Nov. 23 change-log entry says the earlier rise from seven to nine raised the minimum from 391 to 393, while also saying the later updates raised the toll to 11.
3. The Nov. 23 methodology paragraph says the Dymer rise from seven to 11 added four deaths to a minimum of 391, producing 395.
4. The Nov. 23 chronology entry says the Dymer rise from seven to 11 added four deaths to a minimum of 391, producing 395.
5. The Nov. 22 reconciliation text in the method page says the total moved from 379 to 391 (+12), while its incident breakdown and change-log descriptions require line-by-line reconstruction. A separate change-log description uses 389 as the Nov. 22 baseline before the final rise from six to seven takes it to 390. These cannot all be treated as one consistent sequence without checking event inclusion, timestamps and overlapping reports.
6. The Nov. 24 change log and method page both record the rescued woman's later death as one additional death, from at least 407 to at least 408. This appears internally consistent as a later increment, but its cumulative baseline must be checked against the corrected Nov. 23 reconstruction.
