## Records added

Five additional records were entered for Nov. 22:

- Vyshneve: three people killed in the strike on a NOVUS supermarket. The record preserves the reported progression of one death, then two, then three. The final toll is counted once.
- Bila Tserkva district: one man killed in a drone attack on a private house. This is separate from the Nov. 25 fatal attack elsewhere in the district.
- Dnipro: one woman killed in Russian attacks on the city. This is separate from the Nov. 3, Nov. 10 and Nov. 16 Dnipro incidents.
- Novoslobidska community, Sumy region: 15-year-old Kyrylo Kozburh killed when an FPV drone exploded after two residents encountered it near Mazivka. The official age discrepancy (14 in the initial regional statement, 15 in the community head's later identification) is preserved in the source notes.
- Zaporizhia, Zavodskyi district: one 40-year-old man killed when a guided aerial bomb struck an enterprise. The Nov. 22 report of one death in Zavodskyi district and the Nov. 23 police report of the 40-year-old enterprise worker are treated as the same death, not two separate incidents.

The Dymer incident remains a single record with 12 deaths: two already included in the 379 baseline, four additional bodies recovered on Nov. 23 to reach 11, and the rescued woman's later hospital death on Nov. 24 to reach 12.

## Important unresolved total corrections

The full source reconstruction has identified two potential undercounts in the existing method:
- Dnipro, Nov. 3: final source count two, but the method adds one.
- Chornomorsk, Nov. 11: final source count four, but the method adds only the initial one.

These may mean the published minimum of 418 is understated by four, unless the additional deaths were incorporated elsewhere in later reconciliations. The complete cumulative chain must be checked before any correction is made. The number 418 is not being accepted or changed at this stage.
