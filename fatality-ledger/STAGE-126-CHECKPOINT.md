# Stage 126 checkpoint — clarify Kramatorsk Oct. 8 subtotal attribution

Date: Nov. 27, 2026

## Finding

The Oct. 11 ≈80 snapshot labels 33 deaths as the Kramatorsk bus strike. This matches the State Emergency Service figure reported by Interfax-Ukraine on Oct. 8 at 13:21. The same report separately notes three deaths in Bilenke.

Later, stronger incident-by-incident evidence establishes:
- 30 deaths in the Oct. 8 10:00 bus strike, confirmed by Kramatorsk City Council's Oct. 9 recap and an Oct. 26 police response;
- three deaths in a separate 10:10 strike on Bilenke;
- two deaths in a separate 18:40 FPV strike on a civilian car in central Kramatorsk.

The Oct. 9 regional administration's aggregate of 32 killed in Kramatorsk and three in Bilenke is consistent with 30 bus deaths plus two car deaths in Kramatorsk and three separate Bilenke deaths.

## Data-quality correction

The bus incident's previous `incident_description` said the Oct. 11 33-death subtotal row was “reconciled as 30 bus victims plus three in Bilenke.” That was too definite and contradicted the archived public page, which explicitly labels 33 as the bus-strike toll. It also exceeded what the sources establish.

Corrected the description to say that:
- the Oct. 11 row labels 33 as the bus strike, matching the disputed SES figure;
- the later local/police final bus toll is 30;
- Bilenke and the car strike are separate incidents;
- the snapshot does not establish whether Bilenke or car deaths were separately included.

No revision values, incident tolls, source records or effective dates changed.
