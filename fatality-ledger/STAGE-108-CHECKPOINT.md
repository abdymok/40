### Oct. 22 central-market strike — third woman dies in hospital

The market strike killed two women at the scene on Oct. 22. A third woman died in hospital on the morning of Oct. 23, taking the locally reported toll to three.

The Oct. 22 ≈123 snapshot predates the third death. The remaining dated transitions from 137→148 are separately itemized in Stages 54 and 100. None adds the third Lozova market death. The third death is therefore an established exclusion from the Oct. 28 148 baseline and its later 421 carry-forward.

Keep the incident's final documented toll at three. Keep the third-death revision's `ledger_effective_at` null because it was not a dated addition to the public subtotal. The revision reason now records the established exclusion.
