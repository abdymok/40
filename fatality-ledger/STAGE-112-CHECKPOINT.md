# Stage 112 checkpoint — separate Oct. 17 Sloviansk private-sector strike

Date: Nov. 27, 2026

## Finding

The 12 deaths listed by OHCHR for Sloviansk on Oct. 17 are now reconciled to two distinct attacks:

1. **03:40 — two five-storey apartment buildings:** nine people killed after five days of rescue work. This remains the existing incident record `UA-DON-SLOVIANSK-2026-10-17-RESIDENTIAL-AIRSTRIKES`.
2. **About 09:20 — private residential sector:** four FAB-250 bombs hit private homes, damaging 22 houses. The State Emergency Service reported three people killed and two injured. This is a separate incident, now recorded as `UA-DON-SLOVIANSK-2026-10-17-PRIVATE-SECTOR-AIRSTRIKE`.

The official DSNS Telegram channel reported the three deaths. Suspilne Donbas and Ukrainska Pravda separately identify the private-sector strike and distinguish it from the earlier high-rise attack. The combined local toll is nine plus three, matching OHCHR's 12-death representative-incident count. The earlier OHCHR discrepancy is therefore resolved without revising the nine-death incident.
