Date: 26 November 2026

## Baseline snapshot

The relevant Oct. 11 snapshot is commit `733a9b45892d232c5e0e2f2ac120f659ef58002b` (09:00 UTC). It displays approximately 80 civilian deaths and describes the figure as a provisional incident-level subtotal for selected reports in the chronology.

The method paragraph says it counts each incident once, excludes broader citywide or nationwide aggregates where they may overlap with incident-level reports, and is not a comprehensive tally for Ukraine. It specifically warns that the October 7–8 Kyiv reports require caution: two deaths in Darnytsky district, two in a Desniansky residential-building strike, and two later in a Solomiansky shop-building strike. Citywide totals are not added separately.

## Comparison with the current ledger

The current ledger has 14 incident records dated on or before Oct. 11, with a raw final documented-death sum of 93. This is not a like-for-like comparison with the historical approximate 80 because:
- the current ledger uses later final tolls, including the Zaporizhia revision from 20 to 23 and Solomiansky shop revision from two to three;
- it contains incidents that are not individually itemized in the Oct. 11 method note, including later-added records and delayed deaths;
- the historical page warns against adding overlapping citywide and incident-level reports;
- the 80 baseline is an approximate selected-report subtotal, not a complete national or incident census.

No adjustment of the current ledger or the historical public subtotal is warranted from this comparison alone.
