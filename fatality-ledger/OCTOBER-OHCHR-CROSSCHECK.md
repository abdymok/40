# October 2026 OHCHR cross-check leads

**Status:** External cross-check only. No deaths added or changed on the basis of this report alone.  
**Reference:** UN Human Rights Monitoring Mission in Ukraine, *Protection of Civilians in Armed Conflict — October 2026*.

The OHCHR October report provides a useful independent cross-check of the October incident inventory. It reports at least 372 civilian deaths across Ukraine during October. That figure is **not** a replacement for this project's provisional, incident-level subtotal and must not be imported wholesale. Its representative-incident section does, however, identify several events or counts that are absent from or differ from the current chronology archive.

## Additional discrepancy: Kyiv, Oct. 13

Kyiv City Prosecutor's Office and Kyiv Police reporting confirms three women killed in the Desniansky residential building struck on the evening of Oct. 13. The incident record preserves 1 → 2 → 3; the third death was reported by the prosecutor at 08:50 on Oct. 14 and confirmed by police at 11:50. OHCHR's three-death count is therefore matched by local official reporting. The two deaths in Holosiivsky district during the same overnight attack are a separate incident and are recorded separately. The earlier combined ledger record that included both the Desniansky and Holosiivsky deaths has been split to avoid counting the three Desniansky victims twice.

## Lozova market final toll update — Nov. 27

Local reporting now establishes five deaths in the Oct. 22 central-market strike. Two women died at the scene; a third woman died in hospital on Oct. 23; a man died from his injuries by Oct. 24; and another man died in hospital by Oct. 25. Suspilne Kharkiv reported the fourth death citing the mayor. The mayor's official Telegram post and Ukrinform reported the market toll at five, while separately identifying the man killed in the Oct. 24 warehouse strike. The warehouse death is a distinct incident and is not part of the market's five.

The OHCHR October update lists four deaths (two women and two men). The local final count is five (three women and two men). This is a one-death difference in the reported totals and a difference in the sex breakdown, not an unidentified fourth casualty. Preserve both source records and do not force a one-to-one match between individual victims.


## Kherson final toll update — Nov. 27

The Oct. 17 Kherson airstrike sequence killed two men, not one. The first was a 64-year-old emergency medical services driver killed in the repeat strike while responding to the initial attack. A 58-year-old resident who was critically injured at home died in hospital on Oct. 18. MOST reported the death citing Kherson City Military Administration head Yaroslav Shanko; a separate report cited Kherson Regional Military Administration head Oleksandr Prokudin and stated that two people had been killed. OHCHR's October report independently records two men killed in the Kherson airstrikes.

The incident record now preserves 1 → 2. This resolves the local-vs-OHCHR incident toll discrepancy, but not the separate historical-subtotal membership question: the second death occurred before the Oct. 21 ≈116 subtotal, and no surviving update establishes whether it was already in that carried-forward figure or the later 148/421 totals. Its `ledger_effective_at` remains null. No public subtotal is changed.


## Sloviansk two-incident reconciliation — Nov. 27

The OHCHR total of 12 people killed in Sloviansk on Oct. 17 is now matched by two distinct local incidents. The 03:40 attack on two five-storey apartment buildings killed nine people. A separate attack at about 09:20 struck the private residential sector with four FAB-250 bombs, damaging 22 homes and killing three people, with two injured. The official State Emergency Service channel reported the three private-sector deaths; Suspilne and Ukrainska Pravda distinguish this attack from the earlier high-rise strike.

The ledger now has separate records for the nine-death high-rise strike and the three-death private-sector strike. No death is added to the first incident's toll. The private-sector incident's inclusion in the Oct. 21 ≈105 selected subtotal remains unproven because that baseline is not a complete incident roster. Its effective date remains null. This resolves the incident-level 9-versus-12 discrepancy but not the separate historical-subtotal membership question.

## Kramatorsk, Oct. 8 — three incidents, 35 deaths

Kramatorsk City Council's Oct. 8 memorial statement and Oct. 9 incident-by-incident recap distinguish three separate fatal attacks on Oct. 8:
- **10:00, central Kramatorsk bus strike:** 30 killed and 20 injured. The Oct. 26 response from Kramatorsk District Police, reported by Suspilne Donbas, confirms 30 killed and 20 injured in the bus-attack investigation. OHCHR records 11 verified deaths and 19 additional reported deaths pending verification, also totalling 30.
- **10:10, Bilenke:** three people killed in a separate strike. The City Council and Suspilne Donbas identify the three victims in Bilenke.
- **18:40, central Kramatorsk:** an FPV drone struck a civilian car, killing two men.

The City Council's Oct. 9 report gives a community-wide toll of 35: 30 + 3 + 2. An earlier regional report described 33 deaths in the bus attack; the later police response and local itemization support 30 for the bus strike, with the additional three deaths recorded separately in Bilenke. The Oct. 11 ≈80 selected subtotal's Kramatorsk row of 33 is now decomposed as the 30 bus victims plus three Bilenke victims. The two car deaths remain a separate possible historical-inclusion question; they are not added to the 33 row by arithmetic.


## Kramatorsk, Oct. 8 incident split — Nov. 27

The City Council's Oct. 8 memorial statement and Oct. 9 incident-by-incident recap distinguish three fatal attacks in Kramatorsk community: 30 people killed in the 10:00 bus strike in central Kramatorsk; three people killed in a separate 10:10 strike in Bilenke; and two men killed when an FPV drone struck a civilian car in central Kramatorsk at 18:40. The Council's community-wide total is 35.

The 30-death bus toll is corroborated by a later Kramatorsk District Police response dated Oct. 26, reported by Suspilne Donbas. OHCHR records 11 verified deaths and 19 additional reported deaths pending verification, also totalling 30. The earlier regional report of 33 deaths attributed to the bus strike is treated as an overinclusive interim report; the later police response and local incident breakdown separate the Bilenke three. The Oct. 11 ≈80 Kramatorsk row of 33 is decomposed as bus 30 + Bilenke 3. The two car deaths remain a separate historical-inclusion question.
