# Stage 99 checkpoint — DTEK driver Shcherbyna is a separate uncounted fatality

Date: 26 November 2026

## Finding

DTEK driver Vitaliy Shcherbyna was killed when a Russian drone struck a lorry at one of DTEK Energy's coal-mining enterprises in Dnipropetrovsk region overnight on Oct. 25. DTEK's Ukrainian statement and Interfax-Ukraine's Oct. 25 report confirm the death. The company does not disclose the mine's settlement or district; the incident is recorded at regional level only.

The earlier duplicate hypothesis was based on a mistaken description of the Oct. 25 regional casualty roundup. The Dnipropetrovsk Regional Military Administration's Oct. 25, 07:30 update identifies three deaths in the Synelnykove residential strike. Its subsequent update raises that same incident to four after a 62-year-old man dies in hospital. These are not the DTEK lorry-strike victims. The one-death Dnipro incident in the current ledger is dated Oct. 23 and is also separate.

## Historical subtotal test

- The ≈123 baseline on Oct. 22 predates Shcherbyna's death.
- The Oct. 25 123→132 batch is itemized and maps to the Oct. 23 Dnipro death, the four-death Synelnykove strike, and four other named deaths. It does not identify the DTEK incident.
- The Oct. 26 132→137 batch maps to Marhanets, Lozova, Konotop and Yarova.
- The subsequent 137→148 increments map to other identified incidents, including the separate Oct. 26 Pavlohrad death of DTEK miner Valentyn Kotelevskyi.
- No mapped Oct. 25–28 increment includes Shcherbyna.

The DTEK fatality is therefore a distinct documented death, but it is not evidenced in the historical 148 baseline or the published 421 minimum.
