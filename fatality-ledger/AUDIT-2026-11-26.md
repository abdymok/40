## Structural finding

The current site does not yet have a machine-readable incident ledger. Fatality information is embedded in long prose paragraphs in `method/index.html`, repeated in chronology entries, and summarized again in `articles/changes.html`. The dashboard number is hard-coded in the chronology HTML. The same event can therefore have several separately maintained descriptions and cumulative figures.

The existing SOP already states that dashboard counts should be the single source of truth and that methodology notes should reference dashboard values. The implementation does not currently meet that standard.

### The 33 null effective-date entries

The 33 null `ledger_effective_at` values occur across 26 incident records. They are not 33 independent deaths, 33 incidents, or 33 dates that can safely be filled in. They fall into four working groups:

| Group | Revision entries | Treatment |
|---|---:|---|
| Historical subtotal membership unresolved | 18 | The incident or later revision is documented, but the incomplete historical snapshots do not establish whether or when it entered a carried-forward public subtotal. Do not assign an effective date by inference. |
| Interim or superseded toll stages | 11 | Intermediate reports (for example, successive Pryluky, Kramatorsk-bus, Sloviansk and Nova Vodolaha tolls) are not independent additions on top of the final incident toll. |
| Downward correction not reflected in the public subtotal | 1 | Kharkiv market strike, 3 → 2. The public sequence retained the earlier three-death figure; the incident ledger records the corrected local toll. |
| Later final increases with no established public inclusion date | 3 | The third Solomiansky shop-strike death and the two later Chornomorsk hospital deaths are documented in final incident totals, but their later inclusion in the historical public subtotal is not established. |
| **Total** | **33** | |

The 18 historical-membership entries include possible gaps and unresolved overlaps, not confirmed omissions. In particular, the October 11 and October 28 carried-forward snapshots do not provide complete incident membership lists. The absence of an incident from a partial breakdown does not prove it was excluded from the aggregate. Conversely, a documented incident death must not be presumed included merely because a later aggregate is larger.

The null values therefore remain deliberate safeguards. Filling all 33 with incident dates, report dates, or guessed dashboard dates would create false precision and could double-count deaths. The raw 458 ledger sum must remain separate from the 427 public running minimum until each historical-membership question is resolved against contemporaneous records.

## Stage 54 — first historical-membership case review

**Scope:** Earliest three incident records in the 18-entry historical-membership working group.
**Method:** Compare contemporaneous source publication times, the Oct. 11 ≈80 snapshot, and the explicitly mapped Oct. 11–28 subtotal increments. A missing named row is not treated as proof of exclusion from a carried-forward aggregate.

### 1. Kramatorsk residential-building death, Oct. 5 — one woman

The death was reported on Oct. 6 by Ukrinform citing the Donetsk Regional Prosecutor's Office, with corroboration from Ukrainska Pravda and Censor.NET. It predates the Oct. 11 ≈80 snapshot.

The Oct. 11 snapshot's death-bearing rows do not name this incident. The subsequent mapped 80→92 and 123→148 increments also do not identify it. The Oct. 25 123→132 batch is allocated to four deaths in Synelnykove, one in Kharkiv and four deaths reported across other regions on Oct. 23; it is not this Oct. 5 Kramatorsk death.

**Finding:** Historical inclusion in the ≈80, ≈123, ≈148 or later carried-forward public subtotal is not established. The incident is well sourced and remains in the incident ledger. Its null effective date is appropriate. Do not classify it as a confirmed omission or add it to 427.

### 2. Ivano-Frankivsk drone-debris death, Oct. 8 — one security guard

The death was reported on Oct. 9 by Ukrainska Pravda, Ukrinform and Hromadske, citing the mayor and regional administration. It predates the Oct. 11 ≈80 snapshot.

The Oct. 11 snapshot does not itemize Ivano-Frankivsk. The mapped 80→92 sequence and the itemized Oct. 25–28 123→148 increments do not name the incident. No contemporaneous cumulative update recovered in this pass assigns it a place in the running subtotal.

**Finding:** Historical inclusion remains indeterminate. The incident and death are independently corroborated; the uncertainty concerns only whether the death was included in a rounded or carried-forward aggregate. Keep its effective date null and do not add it to 427.

### 3. Unallocated Kyiv morning death, Oct. 8 — one person

Klitschko's 10:52 Kyiv-time update, reported by Ukrainska Pravda and Ukrinform, recorded one death and 11 injured in attacks on the capital before the later Solomiansky shop-building strike. The source does not identify the specific incident or the victim's location.

The Oct. 11 ≈80 snapshot includes a broad one-death Kyiv citywide row but explicitly warns that citywide totals may overlap with incident-level reports and must not be added separately. The source record cannot be matched to a distinct incident-level row. It is therefore not possible to determine whether this is a separate death in addition to the named Kyiv incidents or a citywide summary of one of them.

**Finding:** This is an identity/overlap problem as well as a subtotal-membership problem. Do not count it as an independent addition, do not merge it into another incident without evidence, and retain the null effective date.
