## Two material discrepancies with the current method subtotal

### 1. Dnipro, Nov. 3: two deaths, not one

The method page's Nov. 3 arithmetic adds one death for the morning attack on Dnipro. The source sequence, however, is:
- 09:28: the regional administration initially reports one death.
- 13:38: after the rescue operation, the regional administration reports **two dead and 20 injured**.

The incident record has two deaths and a preserved 1 → 2 revision. Unless the second death was already included elsewhere in the project subtotal, the method's running minimum is understated by one from this point onward.

### 2. Chornomorsk, Nov. 11: four deaths, not one

The method page records one person killed in the market strike and adds one to the cumulative minimum. Subsequent reporting changes the incident toll:
- 13:34: Interfax reports one person killed and 11 injured.
- 14:06: two hospitalized victims die, raising the toll to three.
- Later on Nov. 11: another hospitalized victim dies, raising the toll to **four**.

Ukrainska Pravda's 19:27 report records four deaths. The incident record therefore has four documented deaths and a revision history of 1 → 3 → 4. The method page appears to omit three subsequent deaths unless they were incorporated elsewhere. No such later reconciliation has been identified yet.

These are not cosmetic differences. Together they may make the public running minimum at least four deaths too low, subject to checking for duplicate inclusion elsewhere.
