# Stage 113 checkpoint — Oct. 8–9 corroboration and Kyiv overlap

Date: Nov. 27, 2026

## New source corroboration

An official Ukrainian letter to the United Nations, A/81/436–S/2026/819, dated Oct. 14 and circulated Oct. 15, summarizes the 8–14 October attacks. It states that:
- Oct. 9 attacks killed one person in Zaporizhia and one in Dnipropetrovsk region.
- An Oct. 8 strike in Ivano-Frankivsk reportedly killed one person.

The letter is from Ukraine's Permanent Representative to the UN. It is an official Ukrainian government submission, not an OHCHR verification or an independent UN finding. It has been added as corroboration, with that qualification, to the three matching incident records: Ivano-Frankivsk Oct. 8, Synelnykove district Oct. 9 and Zaporizhia Oct. 9. It does not establish whether any of those deaths entered the Oct. 11 ≈80 subtotal or the later carried-forward 148/421 figures.

## Kyiv, Oct. 8: citywide death is distinct from the shop strike

The previously unallocated Oct. 8 Kyiv morning death is now better distinguished from the Solomiansky shop victims. At 10:52, Mayor Vitali Klitschko reported one person killed and 11 injured in attacks on Kyiv since the start of the day. At 16:10, Kyiv Police reported three deaths in the Solomiansky shop strike and four deaths in the day's Kyiv attack overall. The official 4-versus-3 tally establishes that the morning citywide death is not one of the three shop victims, although the person's precise incident and location remain unidentified.

The Oct. 11 ≈80 snapshot displays a broad one-death Kyiv citywide row. Its methodology explicitly says that broader citywide aggregates are excluded where they may overlap with incident-level reports and are not added separately. That visible row therefore does not establish that the morning death entered the selected subtotal. Nor does the later 148 carried-forward baseline provide a complete incident roster. The historical membership question remains unresolved; the revision stays null.
