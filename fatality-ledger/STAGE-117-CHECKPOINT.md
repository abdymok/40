# Stage 117 checkpoint — Zaporizhia Oct. 10 official corroboration

Date: Nov. 27, 2026

## Finding

Ukraine's Oct. 14 letter to the United Nations (A/81/436–S/2026/819), circulated Oct. 15, states that the Oct. 10 Russian air strike on Zaporizhia killed 23 people, including four children, and injured at least 34.

The letter is an official Ukrainian government submission, not an OHCHR verification. It independently corroborates the local final toll of 23 already recorded in the incident ledger. OHCHR's October report separately lists 21 civilian deaths (15 women, four girls and two men). The difference is retained as an OHCHR verification discrepancy; it is not evidence of two unidentified additional victims and does not justify reducing the local final toll.

The UN letter has been added to the Zaporizhia incident's source list and the 20→23 revision's source references.

## Kramatorsk historical-inclusion qualification

The Oct. 11 ≈80 chronology snapshot explicitly labels the 33 deaths as the Kramatorsk bus strike. Later Kramatorsk City Council itemization and an Oct. 26 police response establish 30 deaths in the bus strike and three in a separate Bilenke attack. The 33 may have conflated those three Bilenke deaths with the bus toll, or may have overstated the bus toll. The surviving snapshot does not establish whether the Bilenke deaths were included. Their `ledger_effective_at` has therefore been returned to null. The two deaths in the separate evening car strike also remain null.
