## Assessment

- The initial two Solomiansky shop deaths are confirmed members of the Oct. 11 approximately-80 selected-incident subtotal (Stage 68).
- The later “at least five earlier deaths reported in Kyiv” phrase is compatible with the six Oct. 7–8 deaths explicitly itemized on Oct. 11. It neither confirms nor disproves the continued inclusion of each incident.
- No reviewed method paragraph or commit diff documents a subtraction of the Solomiansky shop deaths.
- Membership in later 105/116/118 subtotals remains not individually itemized, but there is no positive evidence of removal.
- The third Solomiansky victim, who died later in hospital, remains a subsequent toll revision and must not be projected backwards into the Oct. 11 subtotal.

No arithmetic adjustment is justified. Do not add or subtract the shop deaths from later historical totals.
