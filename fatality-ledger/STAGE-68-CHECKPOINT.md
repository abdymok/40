# Stage 68 checkpoint — October 11 inclusion of Solomiansky shop deaths

Date: 26 November 2026

## Finding

The October 11, 2026 historical snapshot at commit `733a9b45892d232c5e0e2f2ac120f659ef58002b` displayed an approximate selected-incident subtotal of 80 civilians killed. Its method paragraph explicitly states that the subtotal includes:
- two deaths reported in Darnytsky district;
- two deaths in the Desniansky residential-building strike; and
- two deaths in the Solomiansky shop-building strike.

The same paragraph says broader citywide totals may overlap with these incident-level figures and are not added separately. This is direct evidence that the two initially reported Solomiansky shop deaths were included in the Oct. 11 selected-incident subtotal. It resolves the uncertainty left open in Stage 67 for that snapshot.

The current ledger records three final deaths for the Solomiansky shop incident, because a third victim later died in hospital. The Oct. 11 historical subtotal predates that later toll revision and explicitly names the initial two, not the later final three.

## Later 105/116/118 snapshots

The Oct. 21 snapshot at approximately 105, and the Oct. 22 snapshots at approximately 116 and 118, use the grouped wording “at least five earlier deaths reported in Kyiv.” The 105 snapshot's explanatory paragraph does not separately name the Solomiansky shop incident. The 116 and 118 paragraphs retain the same grouped wording.

The five are numerically consistent with the two Oct. 7 Darnytsky deaths, the two Oct. 7 Desniansky deaths, and the one Oct. 8 morning citywide casualty report. That remains a plausible mapping, not a proven one. The Oct. 11 method confirms that the shop's initial two deaths were already included in the earlier selected subtotal, but it does not prove whether they remained in the later 105/116/118 subtotals. The later explanatory paragraphs are selective rather than exhaustive, so omission is not proof of removal.

Accordingly:
- Mark the initial two Solomiansky shop deaths as confirmed members of the Oct. 11 approximately-80 selected-incident subtotal.
- Keep their membership in the later 105/116/118 subtotals unresolved.
- Do not add or subtract the shop deaths from any later historical subtotal.
- Keep the third, later hospital death distinct as a subsequent toll revision; do not project it backwards into the Oct. 11 snapshot.
- Retain Stage 67's 2+2+1 mapping as plausible, not proven.
